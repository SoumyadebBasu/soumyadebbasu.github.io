About Us

Multiple Ad Choices

We belive in the power of choices. In advertising there is no one-size-fits-all approach. Avertising is all about personalizing. Advertising is about bringing your brand to life in the physical world and to reach out to those who walk beyond the glassdoor, the people. That is why we offer you Mutiple Ad Choices.

From our office in Barasat, West Bengal, we are dedicated to bringing your brand to life in your choice of media.
